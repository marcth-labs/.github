### marcth-labs

Engineering work by **[Marc Theriault](https://github.com/marcth)** — Ottawa, Canada.

An organisation rather than a personal account for one specific reason:
demonstrating separation of duties needs two principals and a restriction on
who may push to the default branch, and GitHub only offers that restriction on
organisation-owned repositories.

So this is one person, and the organisation is part of the argument rather than
a claim to be more than one. The repositories here say so themselves.

#### [proving-ground](https://github.com/marcth-labs/proving-ground)

An auditable, agent-implemented, human-approved delivery pipeline. An agent
credential writes and commits; a human credential approves and merges; neither
can do the other's job, and the whole trail verifies offline from a clone.
